03What blocks wave four

One decision, yours

The grid geometry. Your decks measure roughly 5.5% side margins and a three-step top margin — 3.9, 6.7 or 9.6% depending on what leads the slide. The style guide states 6% flat and 7.5% flat. The 14% footer band removes 8% of canvas your original decks use.

The Book can tell you the three-step top margin is a real decision that got flattened to a constant. It cannot tell you which you want. Everything in wave four is measured against whichever you pick, so it is worth answering before we generate twelve layouts against the wrong one.

Seven contradictions inside Style Guide v1.4 are also still open — butter as Series 4, title lines two or three, coverage 55 or 70 per cent, full-bleed accent grounds, logo minimum 90px or 1.5%, logo in running headers, eight tokens or ten. None of them block a wave; all of them are recorded rather than quietly resolved.
