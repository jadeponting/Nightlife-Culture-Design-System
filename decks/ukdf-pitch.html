07 · What UKDF support would change

A clear
step change

Without protected time, commissions shape the work reactively. UKDF lets the public-interest foundation be designed first.

A field-backed argument

A published thesis, 2–3 place cases, and evidence of how nightlife produces agency and place value.

A tested first method

One practical prototype — a value ledger or shared evidence method — and clear learning about what travels.

An investable institution

Operating model, governance, partner commitments and a costed next phase.

What was trimmed, and one new thing

A layout contract caps the words a slot can carry. These lines did not fit, and are parked here for your call, not deleted:

Trimmed Slide 2 original. “I am seeking a fellowship to establish Nightlife Culture and turn this conviction into a tested institution, a practical first method and a fundable next phase.” — the three outcomes now carry this on slide 08, and the ask on 09.
Trimmed Slides 4/5 original. “When value is extracted without reinvestment, the conditions that create the next artist, organiser or enterprise are weakened.” — not placed. It is the strongest warning in the deck; say where it goes and it goes there.
Trimmed Slide 3 original. “Better language, evidence and reinvestment can change what places recognise and back.” — not placed; say where and it goes there.
Trimmed Slide 4 original. “Artists and organisers create the encounters through which people learn to act — and places become distinctive. Dynamism starts earlier: in informal relationships, experiments and gathering places.” — would not fit the ink-strip layout with the band. Candidates: the operating plan, or a second mechanism slide.
Trimmed Slide 6 original. “Trusted across worlds” merged into “A doer, not an observer” — five cards became four so none needs a caption under 16pt.
Trimmed Slide 4/5 duplicate. The original deck contained the talent-programme slide twice. Once here.

One new style, awaiting your sign-off

The catalogue’s cells and columns carried a numeral and a label only. A real pitch needs one sentence under the label — the Why-Carly cards, the three outcomes. So there is now a detail line: Inter regular, sized between the label and slide body, sentence case. It is the only thing on these slides that did not exist yesterday. If it reads wrong, it is one line to change.
